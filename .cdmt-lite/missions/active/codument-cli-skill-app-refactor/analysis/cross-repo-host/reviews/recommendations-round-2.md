## Findings

- **Ownership and extension contracts are explicit.** The complete disposition table assigns one source owner and bounded role per target. Support ports, genuine capsule closures, shell surfaces and the Codument adapter's two public sides are stated. Three new boundaries have installation/lifecycle purposes. Architecture §4 distinguishes command registrations, extensible declarative Kind registrations and the finite executable ABI; it supplies concrete runtime/input/config sketches without claiming they already exist. Domain semantics and readers remain Codument-owned; no new compiler, universal dispatcher or mandatory Actor framework is proposed.
- **Installation, composition and lifecycle are separate obligations.** Architecture §3 and rec-02 demand actual basic-five-package installation closure without resource/Hono/browser/Vue/MCP. T12/T13/T14 and injected optional providers have distinct boundaries. rec-03 separately verifies lazy construction, per-instance state, concurrent initialization, failure/drain/close behavior and borrowed handles. Imports or tree-shaking cannot stand in for package-manager closure evidence.
- **Compatibility does not weaken admission.** Architecture §6 and rec-02 distinguish exact installed name/version/integrity from semantic owner/revision/schema/reader identity. Trusted finite release mappings, preserve-existing-write, exact old-name compatibility or explicit migration, conflict rejection and independent version axes remain required. A renamed package, equal version number or self-declared alias cannot bypass checks. Formal `codument/`, legacy globals and Vue keys retain independent treatment.
- **Execution bytes and source authority stay distinct.** rec-04 retains two deterministic mutation families and the evidence distinction between R04's static window and R05's uncertain dependency-byte guarantee. Source-entry rehash alone cannot pass relative-dependency or metadata/node_modules cases. Captured closure or explicit refusal, instance cleanup and final rec-03/04 receipt agreement are observable acceptance requirements. Journal/recovery material is not promoted into an event-log authority or completed migration ledger.
- **Snapshot modes preserve the stated user boundary.** Architecture §9, D09 and B05 distinguish consumer scaffold, allowlisted source-only export and explicit full eligible-working-tree snapshot. Full mode retains modified tracked files, tracked files matching ignore, nonignored untracked bytes and the original lock; docs/codument/mission/tracked generated paths cannot silently disappear. Rebrand and dependency re-resolution have separate receipts. Provenance does not invent a dirty historical merge base or authorize a second long-term public-source owner.
- **Delivery, dependencies and rollback are reviewable.** rec-01…04 each have the eight required sections, source evidence, bounded outputs, observable positive/negative cases, prerequisites and rollback. Logical dependencies are `rec-01 → rec-02 → {rec-03, rec-04}`, with serial edits for the overlapping runtime/materializer boundary and a joint artifact-set gate before adoption. C06's source ledger is split into rec-01, avoiding the apparent C01/C06 cycle. B01/B02 product adoption, B03 normal transitive resolution and core recipe proof, B04 layered publication obligations, B05 clone, and B07/B08 retained domain/migration/context/full-check debt remain visible. Source rollback protects new user edits; product rollback pins immutable versions; authored-resource rollback requires backup/receipt/controlled restore.
- **Partial gates cannot complete the product.** The two corrected [loop Verify fields](../../../loop.md) require Halfcode canonical-source tests and independent newly packed public-artifact fixtures; pre-adoption Codument tests only provide historical/post-adoption regression evidence. Frozen `init/status/upgrade-workspace`, final entry switching, old src retirement and real dogfood stay behind the separate user decision. Core library/recipe, full Codument native/bin/JSON/exit compatibility, target-OS smoke and real npm publication remain different obligations.
